1. Advance Payments

All bookings require an advance payment to confirm hotel and transport arrangements. This advance is generally non-refundable, as it is passed on to third-party vendors.

2. Refund Eligibility

SituationRefund
Apna Travel Guru cancels the tourFull refund
Traveler cancels before vendor payments are madeRefund minus processing charges
Traveler cancels after vendor payments are madeAs per cancellation policy

3. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

4. How to Request a Refund

Message us on WhatsApp with your booking details to request a refund review.
